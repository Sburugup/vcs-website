# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
UCI students interested in venture capital, entrepreneurship, and finance, plus prospective partners and speakers evaluating the organization. Most users are browsing quickly on laptops or phones between classes, looking for credibility, clarity, and easy next steps to engage.

## Product Purpose
Present VCS at UCI as a credible, professional organization and make key information easy to read and act on. Success means visitors can quickly understand who VCS is, what it offers, and how to join, partner, or attend events without visual friction.

## Brand Personality
Professional, ambitious, approachable. The tone should feel polished and trustworthy, with confident hierarchy and restrained energy. Visual expression should be white-primary, clean, and readable, with subtle purple and yellow references to existing brand identity.

## Anti-references
- Over-boxed layouts where every section is trapped in heavy cards
- Loud gradients, decorative effects, and noisy visual treatments
- Low-density novelty UI that feels template-like instead of credible

## Design Principles
1. Readability first: prioritize scanability, line length control, and clear heading hierarchy.
2. White as foundation: use light surfaces with restrained accent color, not color-heavy backgrounds.
3. Structure over decoration: communicate section boundaries with spacing, rhythm, and subtle dividers before using containers.
4. Professional restraint: remove gimmicky type and visual noise, keep motion and accents purposeful.
5. Action clarity: calls-to-action should be obvious, consistent, and visually distinct from body content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
No strict formal compliance target requested, but readability must improve materially: stronger text contrast, predictable hierarchy, and comfortable measure/spacing for body copy across desktop and mobile.
